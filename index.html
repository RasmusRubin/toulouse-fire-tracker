Toulouse: no active fire in the department

The major fire is 200 km away in Gironde, near Bordeaux. Daily fire-danger level changes — see the scale above.

Full context

As of Wednesday 29 July, Toulouse's daily fire-danger had eased to High ("élevé", orange) from Severe. The préfecture's ban on open flames and spark-risk equipment still applies regardless of the daily colour. Small fires have popped up in and around Toulouse this week and been contained quickly.

The bigger story is the Gironde fire near Bordeaux, which has grown to roughly 42,000 hectares with about 220,000 people evacuated at peak — one of the largest evacuations in French history — though it's still well over 200 km from Toulouse.

A heatwave was forecast to peak around Saturday 1 August, which could push the department back to Severe. Check the Météo-France map above for the current day.

WED 29 JUL — 12:00 (rev. afternoon)

Small fires in and around Toulouse, contained; Gironde fire now much larger and 15 km from Bordeaux

Local ignitions this week have been fast and small — quickly reached and put out by SDIS 31. The regional picture is dominated by the Gironde fire, which is now large enough to be shaping national response and drawing in outside resources.

  • Gironde: ~42,000 hectares burned, ~220,000 evacuated at peak — one of the largest evacuations in French history. Two firefighters died near Mérignac.
  • Newly reported: 150+ homes destroyed at Le Porge; fresh evacuations at Marcheprime and parts of Cestas after the Saumos fire flared up. Authorities still call the Gironde front "très défavorable".
  • Some Gironde evacuees are being housed at the MEETT centre on the edge of Toulouse — the relief effort reaching here, not the fire.
  • Military A400M water bomber deployed, 20 tonnes of retardant per pass.
  • Nationally: 12,500+ fire starts, ~44,000 hectares burned since January 2026.
